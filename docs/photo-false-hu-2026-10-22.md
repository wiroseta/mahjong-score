# Automatic penalties from the final photo

A verified final photo is checked for an invalid winning structure only after that player has been selected as the winner and Hu or Zi Mo has been selected. Matching rereads are required. Uncertain identities, conflicting views, partial exposed sets, implausible tile counts, repeated bonus labels, and grouping search limits do not prove a violation.

A proven false win records the existing balanced False Chicken Hand fine: offender −3, each opponent +1. It persists through the normal score/history save, respects scoring control, and rolls back on failure. Dealer and round remain unchanged. The invalid winner selection is cleared so normal win payments cannot also be recorded accidentally.

The final photo cannot establish claim order. A valid complete hand mixing Chi/Pong below two Fan is therefore not automatically fined. Players remain responsible for checking claim history during play. No claim-history tracking has been added.

Automatic penalties retain structured tile evidence in score history, without saving the photo. Repeated scans cannot charge the same declaration twice, including after a correction or cancellation. Existing manual fines in the active hand also prevent a duplicate charge. Corrections and cancellations use the existing history controls.

Validation: 940 application tests passed, including real photo-message handling, legal mixed hands, special winning shapes, readonly sessions, save failures, duplicate scans, and penalty corrections. The local experimental reader routing is preserved separately from the production default RGB64 reader.
